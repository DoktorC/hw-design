# About this Repository

My research activity directly deals with hardware architectures.
During my bachelor and master studies, I went through some courses about
electronics, digital design and (micro)architectures, but I have (almost) never
designed circuits.

So, at a certain point, having a bit of time at my disposal, I decided to dive
into this topic.

This repo contains solutions to exercises that I invent and exercises coming
from textbooks or other resources.
The repo also (will) also contain some design projects to test my curiosity and
skills.
